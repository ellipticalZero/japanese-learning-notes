# Hiragana

There are a total of 46 characters in hiragana: 5 vowels, 40 characters
representing consonant + vowel combinations, and one standalone nasal
consonant (ん).

## Vowels (母音)

| Hiragana | Romaji | Telugu equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| あ | a | అ |
| い | i | ఇ |
| う | u | ఉ |
| え | e | ఎ |
| お | o | ఒ |

## G row (が行) — voiced K row

Adding a **dakuten** (゛) to a K row character changes the sound from
unvoiced (k) to voiced (g). This same mark is used throughout hiragana to
turn other unvoiced consonants into their voiced pairs (e.g. s → z, t → d,
h → b).

| Hiragana | Romaji | Telugu equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| が | ga | గ |
| ぎ | gi | గి |
| ぐ | gu | గు |
| げ | ge | గె |
| ご | go | గొ |
